# Contributing to Ocean Surface

Thank you for improving Ocean Surface. Keep changes focused, preserve third-party
notices, and run the checks described in [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) for the paths
you touch.

Unless you conspicuously state otherwise when submitting it, you license each
intentional contribution for inclusion in this repository under the project's
`MIT OR Apache-2.0` terms, at the recipient's option, without additional terms
or conditions. This does not override a separate written contribution agreement
or the license of third-party material.

Do not submit material you lack the right to contribute. Identify adapted or
vendored work, preserve its notices, and record asset provenance as required by
[`docs/ASSET_PROVENANCE.md`](docs/ASSET_PROVENANCE.md). Contributions do not
receive rights to Ocean names, logos, wordmarks, application icons, or other
protected brand assets beyond [`TRADEMARKS.md`](TRADEMARKS.md) and applicable
law.
