# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Ocean Surface is for operators and developers supervising one or more local AI-agent executions across projects, workspaces, sessions, tools, and extension-owned subagents. They use it during active work to understand what needs attention, inspect truthful execution state, replay what happened, and move into the authoritative transcript when content or control is needed.

## Product Purpose

Ocean Surface is the shared web, PWA, Chrome-extension, and Tauri interface over the Ocean daemon. It renders daemon-owned state and collects operator intent without becoming a second runtime. Ocean Floor is its observability mode: a legible 1990s-computer-game world driven by real, metadata-safe Observatory snapshot/live/replay events and backed by durable local history.

Success means the operator can answer, at a glance: what is running, how executions are related, what changed, what needs attention, and whether the view is live, replaying, disconnected, or incomplete.

## Brand Personality

Operational, oceanic, alive. Familiar product controls should feel trustworthy and quiet; the Ocean Floor world may feel like a polished late-1990s simulation game, but never at the expense of truthful state, legibility, or speed.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards, metric-card grids, and control-heavy AI cockpit chrome.
- Decorative virtual offices that invent pacing, coffee breaks, fake progress, or inferred relationships.
- Raw global event firehoses, transcript content, secrets, paths, or permission payloads presented as observability.
- Canvas-only interfaces without a complete keyboard- and screen-reader-accessible semantic alternative.
- Pixel art used as a skin over mock data or disconnected from the real daemon contract.
- Rising Tides magenta/purple, legacy teal-mint, gradient text, prompt/cursor/code-glyph logos, and sparkle/AI ornament.

## Design Principles

1. Truth drives spectacle: every actor, relationship, state transition, and attention signal comes from an Observatory fact.
2. Game world and instrument are one product: the world rewards exploration while the semantic list and inspector make it operationally precise.
3. One core, many shells: browser, PWA, extension, Tauri, and compact layouts consume the same reducer and product mode.
4. Observation never becomes hidden authority: Ocean Floor is read-only in v1 and links to existing transcript/control surfaces.
5. Degrade honestly: gaps, disconnects, retention limits, reduced motion, and renderer fallback stay visible and useful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for the semantic product surface. Provide complete keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen-reader labels and list alternatives, forced-colors support, contrast-safe state encoding that does not rely on color alone, coarse-pointer compact behavior, and a reduced-motion mode that removes continuous movement without removing information.
